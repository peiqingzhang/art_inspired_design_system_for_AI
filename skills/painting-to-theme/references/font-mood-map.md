# Font-Mood Mapping Reference

Maps visual moods extracted from paintings/images to Google Fonts recommendations. Each entry has a display font (for headings) and body font (for text). All fonts listed are available on Google Fonts and thus freely usable.

## Warm / Organic / Impressionist
Soft brushstrokes, warm tones, natural light, gentle gradients
- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond (elegant serif, fluid)
- **Body**: Source Serif 4 (readable, warm serif)
- **Alt display**: Playfair Display
- **Alt body**: Lora

## Bold / Expressionist / High-Energy
Strong colors, thick strokes, dramatic contrast, dynamic composition
- **Display**: Archivo Black (heavy, bold sans)
- **Body**: Work Sans (geometric, sturdy)
- **Alt display**: Bebas Neue
- **Alt body**: Barlow

## Minimal / Geometric / Abstract
Clean lines, geometric shapes, limited palette, structured composition
- **Display**: DM Sans (clean geometric sans)
- **Body**: IBM Plex Sans (precise, technical)
- **Alt display**: Outfit
- **Alt body**: Inter (only as body, never for full theme)

## Classical / Renaissance / Formal
Rich detail, traditional composition, ornate elements, deep colors
- **Display**: Libre Baskerville (traditional serif)
- **Body**: EB Garamond (classic body serif)
- **Alt display**: Merriweather
- **Alt body**: Crimson Pro

## Moody / Dark / Dramatic
Dark tones, heavy shadows, mysterious atmosphere, high contrast
- **Display**: Syne (quirky geometric, slightly dark)
- **Body**: Karla (neutral humanist sans, readable on dark)
- **Alt display**: Space Grotesk (use sparingly, popular in AI)
- **Alt body**: Atkinson Hyperlegible

## Playful / Pop Art / Colorful
Bright colors, fun shapes, casual energy, bold graphics
- **Display**: Fraunces (fun optical-size serif)
- **Body**: Nunito (rounded, friendly)
- **Alt display**: Lilita One
- **Alt body**: Quicksand

## Luxurious / Art Deco / Refined
Gold tones, geometric elegance, symmetry, premium feel
- **Display**: Cormorant (refined display serif)
- **Body**: Jost (geometric sans, elegant)
- **Alt display**: Bodoni Moda
- **Alt body**: Manrope

## Industrial / Brutalist / Raw
Concrete textures, monochrome, raw materials, utilitarian
- **Display**: JetBrains Mono (monospace, technical)
- **Body**: Overpass (utility sans)
- **Alt display**: IBM Plex Mono
- **Alt body**: Rubik

## Nature / Botanical / Earthy
Greens, earth tones, organic shapes, natural textures
- **Display**: Gilda Display (natural elegance serif)
- **Body**: Cabin (humanist sans, grounded)
- **Alt display**: Vollkorn
- **Alt body**: Fira Sans

## Ethereal / Dreamy / Soft
Pastels, soft focus, flowing forms, light and airy
- **Display**: Tenor Sans (delicate sans)
- **Body**: Questrial (light, airy sans)
- **Alt display**: Raleway
- **Alt body**: Open Sans (only as body text)

## Japanese / East Asian / Zen
Calligraphic qualities, negative space, balance, ink-like
- **Display**: Noto Serif Display (broad coverage, elegant)
- **Body**: Noto Sans (clean, excellent CJK support)
- **Alt display**: Shippori Mincho
- **Alt body**: M PLUS Rounded 1c

## Retro / Vintage / Nostalgic
Warm faded tones, period typography feel, analog texture
- **Display**: Abril Fatface (bold didone)
- **Body**: Old Standard TT (traditional body)
- **Alt display**: Yeseva One
- **Alt body**: Cardo

## Pairing Principles

1. **Contrast over similarity**: Pair a serif display with a sans body, or a decorative display with a neutral body. Avoid pairing two fonts that look too similar.

2. **Weight hierarchy**: Display fonts can be heavy or decorative; body fonts should be readable at 14-16px.

3. **X-height alignment**: Good pairs share similar x-heights so they look harmonious when used together.

4. **Maximum 2 families**: One display, one body. Adding a third font family rarely improves things and usually creates visual noise.

5. **Test at real sizes**: Display fonts are judged at 24px+; body fonts at 14-16px. A font that looks great at display size may be illegible at body size and vice versa.
